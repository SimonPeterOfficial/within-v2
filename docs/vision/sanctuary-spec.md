# SANCTUARY

### A place to return to yourself.

**Product:** WITHIN
**Experience:** Sanctuary
**Design direction:** Celestial Serenity
**Status:** Proposed design specification
**Visual reference:** Approved Sanctuary concept generated in Phase 01.

*(Founder's design intent, recorded verbatim 2026-11-05. Do not paraphrase into
specs — link this document. The reconciliation in §11+ is derived engineering
context, not founder text.)*

---

## 1. THE EXPERIENCE

Sanctuary is WITHIN's personal space for reflection, self-expression, emotional awareness and personal development.

It should feel different from every other area of WITHIN.

Home is expansive and exploratory.

Sanctuary is intimate, peaceful and personal.

The experience should communicate:

* You can slow down.
* You don't need to have everything figured out.
* Your experiences matter.
* You can express yourself freely.
* Growth happens at your own pace.

Sanctuary must never feel like another productivity application.

### Environmental artwork

The approved concept establishes:

* Floating celestial gardens.
* A distant illuminated palace.
* Waterfalls descending through clouds.
* Reflective water surfaces.
* Natural stone pathways.
* Flowers and lush vegetation.
* Warm sunlight filtering through the environment.
* A circular architectural portal.

The environmental artwork should occupy the majority of the visible screen.

It must remain recognizable throughout the Sanctuary experience.

Different Sanctuary subpages may use different environmental crops or related scenes, but must maintain the same artistic identity.

### Typography

Two complementary type families:

**Display typography**

* Elegant serif.
* Large, expressive headings.
* Generous line spacing.
* Warm ivory colour.

**Interface typography**

* Clean sans-serif.
* Excellent readability.
* Consistent weights.
* Clear distinction between headings, descriptions and metadata.

Exact font selection must be verified against the visual references before implementation.

## 3. SANCTUARY MAIN SCREEN

The main Sanctuary screen is the entry point to the entire experience.

### A. Navigation

The left navigation rail should retain the WITHIN Home design.

It contains:

* WithIn identity.
* Home.
* Sanctuary (active).
* Mirror.
* Connections.
* Studio.
* Worlds.
* Within Time.

The active Sanctuary item receives a warm golden illumination.

### B. Top utility bar

Contains:

* Universal search.
* Notifications.
* User avatar.
* Profile dropdown.

It must remain visually consistent with Home.

### C. Hero section

Main heading:

"You can slow down here."

Supporting text:

"Take a breath. Leave the noise behind for a moment. This space is yours."

The heading should be the primary visual focal point.

### D. Welcome note

A floating glass message in the upper-right area:

"Your sanctuary. Your peace. Your space."

The illustrated celestial character is retained as a visual element pending a decision on whether it represents a recurring WITHIN guide.

### E. Seven primary destinations

These cards must retain the exact sequence established in the concept.

**01 — Reflection**

Pause. Think. Gain clarity.

A place to explore thoughts, experiences and questions.

**02 — Journal**

Write your story, one thought at a time.

Private personal writing with dated entries and optional prompts.

**03 — Emotional Space**

Feel. Express. Understand.

A space for emotional check-ins, expression and gentle reflection.

**04 — Meditation**

Find peace in the present moment.

Guided breathing, quiet moments and relaxation experiences.

**05 — Personal Growth**

Dream. Plan. Evolve.

Personal goals, learning, discoveries and reflections.

**06 — Memories**

Keep what matters.

Preserve meaningful moments, personal notes and selected memories.

**07 — Daily Check-in**

Reconnect with yourself.

An optional, gentle daily reflection experience.

Each card contains:

* Individual environmental artwork.
* Icon badge.
* Title.
* Short description.
* Directional navigation button.
* Hover and focus states.

### F. Bottom-left reminder

A floating audio/reminder panel.

It contains:

* Audio-style icon.
* Short supportive message.
* Playback control.

Audio must be optional, with clear play/pause controls and volume preferences.

### G. Bottom-right journey indicator

A floating personal journey panel.

It can eventually show meaningful user-selected milestones or reflections.

It must not become a streak counter, compulsory progress tracker or engagement pressure mechanism.

## 4. INDIVIDUAL SANCTUARY EXPERIENCES

Each destination opens its own dedicated experience.

### 4.1 REFLECTION

Purpose: Help people explore their thoughts and experiences.

Features:

* Guided reflection prompts.
* Open-ended writing.
* Optional reflection categories.
* Personal notes.
* Searchable reflection history.
* Ability to save or delete entries.

Visual direction:
A quiet garden overlooking a reflective pool.

Main heading:
"Give your thoughts some room."

### 4.2 JOURNAL

Purpose: Give users a private place to document their lives.

Features:

* Create journal entries.
* Rich-text editing.
* Date-based organization.
* Search.
* Optional tags.
* Personal collections.
* Edit and delete controls.

Visual direction:
A beautiful celestial library with warm lighting, floating books and an intimate writing desk.

Main heading:
"Every story deserves a place."

Privacy is essential. Journal entries must not automatically become public or be used for personalization without appropriate user control.

### 4.3 EMOTIONAL SPACE

Purpose: Provide a welcoming environment for emotional awareness.

Features:

* Optional mood check-ins.
* Emotion vocabulary.
* Personal notes.
* Reflection prompts.
* Historical emotional patterns.
* Links to appropriate support resources when needed.

Visual direction:
A peaceful garden with soft violet lighting and a glowing reflective pool.

Main heading:
"Whatever you're feeling, there's room for it."

This is not a diagnostic or therapy service.

### 4.4 MEDITATION

Purpose: Offer quiet, guided experiences.

Features:

* Guided breathing.
* Relaxation audio.
* Quiet ambient environments.
* Session preferences.
* Optional timers.
* Accessibility controls.

Visual direction:
A secluded meditation pavilion overlooking waterfalls.

Main heading:
"Find a moment of stillness."

### 4.5 PERSONAL GROWTH

Purpose: Help users explore who they want to become.

Features:

* Personal goals.
* Learning interests.
* Milestones.
* Progress reflections.
* Personal challenges.
* Growth history.

Visual direction:
A celestial garden with pathways leading toward distant illuminated architecture.

Main heading:
"Become, at your own pace."

Growth should be personal rather than competitive.

### 4.6 MEMORIES

Purpose: Preserve experiences that matter.

Features:

* Personal memory entries.
* Photographs.
* Written moments.
* Dates and optional locations.
* Collections.
* Search and filtering.
* Export and deletion controls.

Visual direction:
A warm memory garden with illuminated photographs and floating light particles.

Main heading:
"Keep the moments that make you."

### 4.7 DAILY CHECK-IN

Purpose: Provide a gentle opportunity to reconnect with oneself.

Features:

* Optional daily reflection.
* Mood selection.
* Short prompts.
* Personal notes.
* Skip option.
* Historical entries.

Visual direction:
A sunrise garden with warm sunlight and a peaceful water feature.

Main heading:
"How are you arriving today?"

No compulsory check-ins or guilt-inducing reminders.

## 5. INTERACTION DESIGN

All seven destinations must share consistent interaction principles.

### Navigation

* Smooth page transitions.
* Clear active destination.
* Consistent back navigation.
* Keyboard accessibility.

### Cards

* Subtle hover illumination.
* Gentle elevation.
* Clear clickable boundaries.
* Accessible focus indicators.

### Glass panels

* Controlled background blur.
* Translucent surfaces.
* Fine illuminated borders.
* Consistent corner radii.

### Motion

* Slow, atmospheric transitions.
* Subtle environmental movement.
* Gentle appearance animations.
* Reduced-motion support.

Motion must never interfere with reading or navigation.

## 6. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

### Desktop

The full atmospheric environment, left navigation, utility bar and seven-card composition.

### Tablet

* Adapted navigation.
* Two-column or three-column destination cards.
* Repositioned floating panels.
* Preserved hero hierarchy.

### Mobile

* Dedicated mobile navigation.
* One-column or horizontally browsable destination cards.
* Simplified hero composition.
* Touch-friendly controls.
* No miniature desktop sidebar.

The mobile experience must feel intentionally designed rather than compressed.

## 7. PRIVACY AND PERSONAL CONTROL

Sanctuary contains highly personal experiences.

Therefore:

* Personal writing is private by default.
* Users control what is saved.
* Users can edit and delete their information.
* Sensitive reflections must not be exposed in public profiles.
* Notifications must avoid revealing private content.
* Personalization must respect user permissions.
* Data export and deletion must be supported.

The feeling of safety must be supported by actual engineering, not just attractive design.

## 11. RECONCILIATION WITH THE APPLICATION (derived, 2026-11-05)

Below is engineering context, not product vision. Product decisions above stand;
where this section conflicts, the founder text wins and the facts here update.

### Where Sanctuary stands today

- `/sanctuary` (`src/app/sanctuary/page.tsx`) is a 5-line redirect to `/home`;
  the comment reads "one route, one name."
- `/home` is the de-facto sanctuary: it hosts `SanctuaryPanel`
  (`src/components/home/SanctuaryPanel.tsx`) — a 5-room list: Journal →
  `/mirror`, Memories → `/photography`, Mood → `/mirror#mood` (dead anchor),
  Voice Notes → `/sanctuary` (back to the redirect), Private Auri → orb.
- The spec's two-room intent (Home = expansive; Sanctuary = intimate) means
  `/sanctuary` must become a real room, not stay a redirect.

### Destination mapping — existing truth vs. gap

| # | Spec destination | Already exists (single source of truth) | Actual gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Reflection | `mirrorEntries` + `src/lib/mirror.ts` + `/api/mirror` + `/api/mirror/[id]` (owner-only at query level); full CRUD UI `src/components/mirror/MirrorExperience.tsx` — **built, unmounted** | Mount MirrorExperience; prompt-led posture |
| 02 | Journal | Same system. `SanctuaryPanel` and landing copy already call Mirror "Journal" | Free-writing/dated posture of the same engine — **not** a second table or API |

| 04 | Meditation | `src/lib/within-sound.ts` (opt-in Web Audio, master off), `/music` audio catalog, content type `"audio"` | The breathing/ambient experience itself; no table required initially |

### Duplication guards (hard rules)

A naive build of §8 would create forbidden parallel systems. Do not build
seconds of any of these — extend or mount the first:

1. **Shell/hero** — `UniverseShell` (has `preset="sanctuary"` atmosphere),
   `WorldHero`, `AppShell`/`TopBar`/`Sidebar`/`BottomNav`, `AuriOrb`.
   `SanctuaryHero.tsx` already exists (orphaned) — wire or delete, never supersede.
2. **Journal engine** — `mirrorEntries` is the one writing system (AGENTS.md:
   "Mirror — owner-only, private by construction").
3. **Mood engine** — `lib/mood.ts` + `--mood-rgb`; check-ins must use these
   ten moods, never a new enum or a second ambient-color system.
4. **Daily ritual** — `DailyReflection` prompts; check-in composes them.
5. **Continuity moment** — `WhileYouWereAway` + `/api/away`, finished and
   unwired; the audit's fix is to mount, not rebuild.
6. **Auri presence** — `AuriOwl`/`AuriOrb`/`AuriPanel` +
   `AuriContextualPresence` (already maps `/sanctuary → sleeping`).
   Two orphaned duplicates exist — adding a third is a build failure.
7. **Keeps** — `saves` + shelves + `MemoryCards` + Trace; a memories table is
   justified **only** for authored user entries (D-3), never as a capture layer.
8. **Nav** — `UNIVERSE_NAV` (`src/lib/navigation.ts`) is the single nav truth:
   sidebar, bottom nav, and command palette all derive from it.

### Compliance callouts (spec × PRODUCT LAWS)

- The spec is already law-compliant in the places that matter most: no streaks
  (§3.G, §4.7), private by default (§7), skip options, no guilt mechanics,
  reduced motion (§5), "not a diagnostic service" (§4.3).
- "Historical emotional patterns" (§4.3/§4.7 history) is permitted **only** as
  the person's own check-ins rendered back to them, invitation-first,
  owner-only. No inference, no categorization of the person (Law 13).
- "Mood selection" must write what the user chose and nothing more — schema
  comment on `mirrorEntries.moodId`: "user-chosen, not inferred."
- Support-resource links (§4.3) must be discovery surfaces, never triggered by
  inferred condition monitoring (Law 17: safety without surveillance).
- §7's "notifications must avoid revealing private content" — notifications
  already exist as a real system; check message bodies before any preview copy.
- Audio panels (§3.F) default silent; "Settings → Sound" master-off already
  exists and always wins.

### Visual identity reconciliation

- `.world-sanctuary` atmosphere already exists (`globals.css` L~1839):
  violet–blue–soft-silver world personality. The spec's "deep twilight violet /
  celestial gold" extends this token family — as `--within-*` additions, never
  hardcoded literals (established convention from the GENESIS handoff).
- Serif display + sans interface is already the house pairing (Fraunces +
  Geist, AGENTS.md design language); §2's "verify against visual references"
  resolves to this pair unless the concept contradicts it.
- Audit `docs/audit-2026-10-27.md` §K-3 names fabricated-data traps
  (fake percentages, fake now-playing, fakeMoonlight Bay) — Sanctuary
  surfaces must not reintroduce any of them.

## 12. DECISIONS REQUIRED (from the human)

- **D-1 · Reflection vs Journal vs Mirror.** Recommend: one writing system
  (`mirrorEntries`), two postures — Reflection = prompt-led entry moment,
  Journal = dated browse/collection view. Mirror remains its own top-level
  room; the Sanctuary cards deep-link into Mirror modes.
- **D-2 · Emotional Space vs Daily Check-in.** Recommend: one check-in act and
  one mood-entry table; "Daily Check-in" is the gentle once-a-day invitation
  (skippable, never guilt-driven) into the same engine that Emotional Space
  presents fully.
- **D-3 · Memories model.** Recommend: authored memory entries (own text +
  date) that **reference** existing truth (saves, creations, mirror moments)
  via the `journeyItems` sourceType pattern — Law 15: keeping is a choice;
  nothing auto-captured.
- **D-4 · Left navigation.** §3.A lists 8 items including **Worlds**, which
  has no route, and "Within Time" (today: `/time` + Atlas with
  `copy.atlas.eyebrow = "Within Time"`). UNIVERSE_NAV deliberately carries 17
  existing routes — "linking to nothing is worse than omitting." Recommend:
  Sanctuary pages render from the shared UNIVERSE_NAV with Sanctuary active +
  warm illumination; a global 8-item nav restructure is a separate product
  decision, not a Sanctuary-side fork.
- **D-5 · Visual reference asset.** The approved Phase 01 concept is not in
  the repo. It must be added (e.g. `docs/assets/sanctuary/`) before §9's
  visual acceptance review can be adjudicated — no headless browser exists in
  this environment, so visual QA is manual against that reference.
- **D-6 · Meditation persistence.** Recommend: ship stateless first (breathing,
  ambient, timer; device-level session prefs), add a table only when the
  product truly needs saved sessions — NO streaks or session history pressure.

### Suggested sequencing

- **P1 — Wire the orphans (audit §L P1 discipline: wire, then delete parallels).**
  Mount `MirrorExperience`, `DailyReflection`, `MoodOrbit` surface,
  `WhileYouWereAway` in `/home`; resolve `WithinExperience` vs panels.
- **P2 — Make `/sanctuary` a real room.** `UniverseShell preset="sanctuary"` +
  `SanctuaryHero` ("You can slow down here.") + destination grid reusing the
  real rooms; silence the `/mirror#mood` dead anchor and the Voice Notes
  dead link (decide: real voice feature or remove entry).
- **P3 — New truth, once.** Mood check-in table + API (D-1/D-2), memory
  entries (D-3), and only then any goals decision (D-6 / journeys-first rule).
