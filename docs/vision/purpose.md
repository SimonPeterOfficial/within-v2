# WITHIN — Purpose

Repository: https://github.com/SimonPeterOfficial/within-v2
Artifact: docs/vision/purpose.md

Canonical statement of what WITHIN is for, in the founder's words.
Do not paraphrase it into specs — link it. The condensed version in
`AGENTS.md` (§ WITHIN PURPOSE) must stay faithful to this text.

---

WITHIN exists to become more than an application, a website or a digital companion.

It exists to become a meaningful part of people's lives.

A place where scattered pieces come together, unanswered questions find direction,
possibilities become experiences and different chapters of life find continuity.

A place that understands that no two people are the same, that everyone carries
different experiences, dreams, challenges and ambitions.

WITHIN grows with its people. It adapts as they change, evolves as their needs
develop and remains a space where they can discover, create, learn, organize,
connect, express and simply be themselves.

It doesn't dictate who someone should become. It gives them room to discover who
they are and who they want to be.

Its purpose is not to replace the world outside, but to help people navigate,
understand and experience that world more fully.

Ultimately, we want someone to look back and say:

> "WITHIN was the missing part in my life. It bridged the gaps, helped me find
> answers, stood by me through different chapters and became more than a
> companion. It became part of my journey, my experiences and the way I navigate
> my world."

**WITHIN isn't here to become someone's entire world. It's here to help make
their world feel more complete.**

---

## How this binds engineering decisions

This section is derived guidance (not founder text). It gives the statement
teeth through the PRODUCT LAWS in `../../AGENTS.md`:

- **Chapters, not sessions.** The test for any feature is which chapter of a
  life it serves — and whether it sends the person back *outside*. A feature
  that keeps someone inside WITHIN violates the founding sentence.
- **Adaptation without surveillance.** "Grows with its people" is true only
  when adaptation is built on what the user chose to keep (Law 15), never on
  tracked behavior (Law 17).
- **Answers, not magic.** "Helped me find answers" must be literal: the system
  may never fabricate the direction it offered (Law 1).
- **No defined destinations.** Success is the person discovering who they are,
  so no labels, diagnostics, or prescribed next steps (Law 13).
- **The retrospective is a measure of trust, not a retention metric.** The
  "look back and say" paragraph describes years of earned trust (Laws 3–4),
  not an engagement goal.
